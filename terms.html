1. What the app does

The app runs on the operator's Windows computer. It turns the operator's videos into short vertical clips and sends each clip to the drafts inbox of the operator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. The app never publishes on TikTok by itself: the operator opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, edits the caption and chooses whether to post it.

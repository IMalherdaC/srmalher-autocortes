1. Data the app accesses on TikTok

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID and display name. This is used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video upload (scope video.upload): the clip files chosen by the operator are sent to the account's drafts inbox.
  • Access tokens: the access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues after the operator authorizes the app.

The app does not read followers, messages, comments, likes, contacts or any other user's data.

2. Where the data is kept

Tokens and settings are stored only on the operator's own computer, inside the app folder. The app has no server and no database of its own. Data is sent only to the APIs needed to do the job: TikTok to upload drafts, and YouTube and Instagram to publish on the operator's own accounts. Spoken text transcribed from the operator's videos may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to pick highlights and write captions. No TikTok account data is sent to that service.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share personal data.
  • It does not show ads, use tracking or analytics, or set cookies. This page sets none either.
  • It does not access the account of anyone other than the operator.

4. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the app. To revoke access at any time, go to TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. The operator can also delete the app's dados folder. Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult operator.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, send a direct message to @srmalher.gta6 on TikTok or Instagram.
